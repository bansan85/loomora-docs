Observed while looking for a way to switch the scheme in headless Chrome (not kept):

  --blink-settings=preferredColorScheme=1 (alone)                     screenshot byte-identical to the light one
  --blink-settings=preferredColorScheme=1,forceDarkModeEnabled=false  byte-identical to the light one
  --force-dark-mode --blink-settings=preferredColorScheme=1           byte-identical to the light one (only
      the combination: --force-dark-mode alone gave the dark page; why the combination did not was not
      investigated)
  --force-dark-mode (alone)                                           the dark page (kept)

So in this headless Chrome 157 only --force-dark-mode switched the scheme; the --blink-settings setting did
nothing. typing-check-log.txt shows the other way, Emulation.setEmulatedMedia (prefers-color-scheme dark
and light) through the DevTools protocol, with the computed colours and the contrast ratios.
